ABOUT THIS SCANNER

What each column means and where the numbers come from.

Indicators
🔥 Big Candle 15 min Flags a bar whose high-to-low range is at least 1.5× the average of the last 10 bars. Green when it closed above its open.
⚡ MACD 1 hour 12/26 EMA difference against its own 9-period signal line. Bullish while MACD sits above the signal.
📈 DOW Breakout 15 min BUY when price clears the highest high of the previous six bars, SELL when it breaks the lowest low.
⚔️ EMA Crossover 5 min Golden while the 20 EMA is above the 50 EMA, Death when it is below.
📊 Bollinger Band 15 min Up when price reaches the upper band (20 SMA plus two standard deviations).
📉 RSI 15 min Classic 14-period Relative Strength Index. Below 30 is oversold, above 70 overbought. The trend column compares it with the previous bar.
🎯 DMI / ADX 15 min 14-period directional movement. DMI is bullish while +DI leads −DI; ADX above 25 means the trend has real strength.
Score Screener Bullish signals minus bearish ones across seven indicators, so it ranges from −7 to +7. Used to sort the screener.

How the data works

Prices come from Yahoo Finance and are delayed, not a live exchange feed.

A background job refreshes all {{ universe }} symbols every {{ refresh_seconds }} seconds in one batch and caches the result, so pages load instantly and never wait on the data provider.
